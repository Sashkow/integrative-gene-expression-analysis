# Why Not STAR Protocols

STAR Protocols requires a companion paper published in a Cell Press journal (Cell, Cell Reports, iScience, etc.). Our prior work appeared in *Biotechnologia Acta* and *Biopolymers and Cell*, which are not Cell Press journals — this is a disqualifying eligibility criterion and would result in a desk reject. Additionally, the manuscript's core contribution is a novel method (softImpute for block-structured missing data + hard masking in limma), not a step-by-step reproduction of an already-published analysis, making it a poor format fit for a protocols journal.
